## Chrome Web Store

**Name:** Hide Profile: Blur Profile Pictures

**Summary** (max 132 chars)

> Blur, hide or replace profile pictures on X, LinkedIn, Peerlist & more. Read the post, not the profile.

**Category:** Social & Communication (or Accessibility)

**Description**

> Faces change how we read. Hide Profile takes profile pictures out of your social feeds so ideas stand on their own.
>
> STYLES
> • Blur: soften every avatar
> • Hide: replace with a neutral placeholder
> • Replace: use a preset avatar or upload your own
>
> SHIELD (optional)
> • Clean language: masks swear words in English and Hindi, including look-alikes like f*ck
> • Blur media: blurs post images and videos until you hover
>
> MORE
> • Reveal on hover to peek at the original
> • Smart detection catches round avatars on redesigned pages
> • Per-site toggles; Alt+Shift+H to turn everything on or off
> • Light and dark popup
>
> WORKS ON
> X / Twitter, LinkedIn, Peerlist, GitHub, Reddit, YouTube, Instagram, Threads, Facebook, Bluesky, DEV, Medium
>
> PRIVACY
> No network requests, no analytics. Settings stay on your device. Open source (MIT).

**Permission justification**
- `storage`: saves your settings and uploaded avatar locally.
- Host access (listed sites): needed to restyle profile pictures on those pages.

**Single purpose:** Hides or replaces profile pictures, and optionally offensive words and media, on social websites.
